### **Ayre by Karma** opens in Miami with modern Indian concept

**Ayre by Karma**, from the team behind Washington, D.C.'s **Karma Modern Indian**, debuted in South Florida last December and is now drawing Resy feature coverage for its contemporary take on the cuisine. The opening marks the group's first expansion outside the D.C. market.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/05/ayre-by-karma-miami/))

### **Dean's** brings British pub cooking to Soho

**Jess Shadbolt** and **Annie Shi** — the team behind decade-old bistro **King** — opened **Dean's** next door, serving stargazy pie, fish and chips, and other classic British dishes. The opening follows a ten-year run for their original restaurant in the same Manhattan neighborhood.

([Eater](https://www.eater.com/video/962739/deans-british-pub-nyc-restaurant-opening-now-open))

### USDA finalizes rule allowing whole milk in all school meal programs

The Department of Agriculture issued regulations effective June 2026 permitting whole milk across federal school nutrition programs, including breakfast and daycare, implementing the **Whole Milk for Healthy Kids Act** signed in January. The rule reverses decades of fat-content restrictions on subsidized school meals.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/05/18/usda-makes-whole-milk-available-in-all-school-meal-programs/))

### **Pow Wow Grounds** fed protesters during Minneapolis ICE raids

**Robert Rice** (White Earth Ojibwe) used his South Minneapolis coffee shop, open since 2010, to prepare and distribute fry bread to community members and protesters after immigration enforcement actions in the area last month. The shop has long served as a gathering place for the Twin Cities Indigenous community.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/05/18/how-fry-bread-fed-minneapolis-during-the-ice-raids/))
