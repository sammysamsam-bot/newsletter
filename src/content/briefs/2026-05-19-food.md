### **Ayre by Karma** opens in Miami with modern Indian concept

**Ayre by Karma**, from the team behind Washington, D.C.'s **Karma Modern Indian**, debuted in South Florida last December with a contemporary take on Indian cuisine. The restaurant is part of **Karma Hospitality Group**'s expansion beyond its D.C. base.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/05/ayre-by-karma-miami/))

### **JenChan's** anchors Cabbagetown, Atlanta

**JenChan's** has become a neighborhood fixture in Atlanta's Cabbagetown, operating limited weekly service (currently Tuesday starts) as a community-focused spot. The restaurant's model emphasizes regulars-over-tourism at a time when neighborhood spots face rising rent pressure.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/05/jenchans-atlanta-cabbagetown/))

### USDA authorizes whole milk in all federal school meal programs

The U.S. Department of Agriculture finalized regulations allowing whole milk across all federal school nutrition programs — including breakfast and day care — effective June 2026. The move implements the **Whole Milk for Healthy Kids Act** signed by President Trump in January, reversing decades of fat-restricted guidelines.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/05/18/usda-makes-whole-milk-available-in-all-school-meal-programs/))

### **Pow Wow Grounds** fed Minneapolis during ICE raids

**Robert Rice** *(White Earth Ojibwe)* and his South Minneapolis coffee shop **Pow Wow Grounds**, opened in 2010, mobilized to provide fry bread and meals during recent ICE operations in the Twin Cities.
