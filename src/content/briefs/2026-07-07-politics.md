### Modi and Prabowo sign defense and cooperation agreements

Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto met in Jakarta and signed up to eight agreements covering defense, health, education, and technology on Tuesday.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-07-07/modi-and-prabowo-seek-to-strengthen-india-indonesia-ties-video))

### French court rules on Le Pen election ban

A French court issued its decision Tuesday on whether Marine Le Pen is barred from running in next year's presidential election. The National Rally leader was sentenced in March to a five-year election ban and a two-year suspended jail term for misusing roughly €474,000 in European Parliament funds between 2009 and 2016.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-07-07/court-to-decide-whether-le-pen-can-run-for-president-video))

### German prosecutors file charges in €300 million payment fraud

German prosecutors filed their first charges in "Operation Chargeback," a global scam that allegedly defrauded millions of credit card holders of roughly €300 million ($343 million) with the involvement of payment company executives.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-07/germany-files-charges-in-operation-chargeback-payment-scam))
