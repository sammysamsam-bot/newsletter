### **Bertrand Grébaut**, influential French chef, dies at 44

**Bertrand Grébaut**, chef-founder of **Septime** in Paris, has died at 44. His vegetable-forward approach at Septime, which opened on the Right Bank in 2011, was credited with shifting the trajectory of modern French cooking.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/05/dining/bertrand-grebaut-dead.html))

### **All Well** opens in Chicago with "casual prix fixe" format

**All Well**, from the team behind **Goodbye Horses**, debuted in Chicago as part of Eater's "Now Open" series, betting on a set-menu model at accessible price points. The opening is one of several 2026 debuts being tracked in Chicago, New York, and D.C.

([Eater](https://www.eater.com/pre-shift/972897/all-well-chicago-restaurant-pre-shift))

### **Oberon** opens at the New Museum in New York

**Oberon**, a full-service restaurant pairing **OMA** architecture with **Julia Sherman**'s plant-focused cuisine, debuted at the newly expanded **New Museum** on the Lower East Side. The space features custom furnishings by **Minjae Kim**.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/30/dining/nyc-restaurant-news.html) · [Galerie Magazine](https://galeriemagazine.com/the-new-museum-debuts-a-cozy-design-forward-dining-destination/))

### **Brian Calvert** named editorial director at **Civil Eats**

**Brian Calvert**, former editor-in-chief of **High Country News**, will lead **Civil Eats** as editorial director. During his tenure at High Country News, he oversaw the establishment of an award-winning Indigenous Affairs team.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/07/06/longtime-environmental-journalist-brian-calvert-to-lead-civil-eats-as-editorial-director/))

### Food runner compensation questioned as delivery shifts role

A **Eater** dispatch examines how the proliferation of delivery and takeout orders has expanded food-runner responsibilities—often without corresponding pay adjustments—at restaurants including a Brooklyn Vietnamese spot. The piece asks whether wage structures have kept pace with the job's evolution.

([Eater](https://www.eater.com/dining-out/972882/how-much-do-food-runners-get-paid-delivery-takeout-orders))

### Banana flavor surges across menus and retail

Banana has emerged as a dominant flavor trend across café menus, snack aisles, and beverage fridges, with the NYT asking whether it has reached "pumpkin spice" level saturation. The piece tracks the flavor's spread from fresh fruit to processed applications.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/06/dining/food-drink-banana-flavor.html))
