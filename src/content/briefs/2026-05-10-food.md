### DOJ settles poultry price-fixing case with **Agri Stats**

The Department of Justice proposed a settlement Thursday in its antitrust case against the data company, which was accused of enabling poultry producers to coordinate pricing and reduce competition. The case was filed in 2023 under the Biden administration.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/05/08/doj-settles-poultry-price-fixing-case-with-agri-stats/))

### Critics say new USDA SNAP rule will not increase healthy options

Retailers accepting SNAP benefits must now double the variety of foods they stock in-store under a final USDA rule, but public health advocates contend the requirement is too broad to meaningfully boost nutritious choices.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/05/07/critics-say-new-snap-rule-will-do-little-to-increase-healthy-options/))

### **Bar Jamón** opens in San Francisco's Opera District

A two-time **James Beard** finalist opens a Spanish tapas and gin bar one block from Davies Symphony Hall.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/bar-jamon-sf/))

### **Simpson's in the Strand** returns in London

Restaurateur **Jeremy King** and **Studio Shayne Brady** revive the nearly 200-year-old dining landmark with silver-trolley roasts and restored Edwardian interiors.

([Galerie Magazine](https://galeriemagazine.com/london-institution-simpsons-in-the-strand-returns-to-its-edwardian-glory/))
