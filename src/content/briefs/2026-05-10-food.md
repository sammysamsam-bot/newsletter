### DOJ settles poultry price-fixing case with **Agri Stats**

The Department of Justice proposed a settlement Thursday with **Agri Stats**, the data company accused of enabling poultry producers to coordinate price hikes and supply decisions. The 2023 Biden-era antitrust case alleged the firm facilitated collusion that reduced competition across the chicken industry.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/05/08/doj-settles-poultry-price-fixing-case-with-agri-stats/))

### USDA's new SNAP retailer rule draws criticism for weak healthy-food mandate

Retailers accepting SNAP benefits must now double in-store food varieties under a finalized USDA rule, but public health advocates say the requirement doesn't specify nutritional quality and will do little to expand access to healthy options. The rule defines minimum stocking standards without mandating fresh produce or whole-food categories.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/05/07/critics-say-new-snap-rule-will-do-little-to-increase-healthy-options/))

### **Simpson's in the Strand** returns in London

Restaurateur **Jeremy King** and **Studio Shayne Brady** have revived the nearly 200-year-old British dining landmark with silver-trolley roasts, restored Edwardian interiors, and a renewed formal dining program.

([Galerie Magazine](https://galeriemagazine.com/london-institution-simpsons-in-the-strand-returns-to-its-edwardian-glory/))

### **Bar Jamón** opens in San Francisco's Opera District

A two-time James Beard finalist has opened **Bar Jamón**, a Spanish tapas and gin bar one block from Davies Symphony Hall, bringing focused cocktail bar culture to the neighborhood.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/bar-jamon-sf/))

### **Border Town** graduates from pop-up to brick-and-mortar

The breakfast taco stand, previously roving, has found a permanent location after building a following through pop-up service.
