### DOJ settles poultry price-fixing case with **Agri Stats**

The Department of Justice proposed a settlement Thursday in its 2023 antitrust case against the data company, which prosecutors accused of enabling poultry producers to coordinate pricing and reduce competition. Terms were not immediately disclosed.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/05/08/doj-settles-poultry-price-fixing-case-with-agri-stats/))

### USDA final SNAP rule doubles in-store food varieties but critics say it won't boost healthy options

Retailers accepting food assistance must now stock twice as many food varieties under a new USDA rule, though public health advocates told Civil Eats the requirement is too broad to meaningfully increase nutritious choices.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/05/07/critics-say-new-snap-rule-will-do-little-to-increase-healthy-options/))

### **Simpson's in the Strand** returns in London after restoration

Restaurateur **Jeremy King** and **Studio Shayne Brady** revived the nearly 200-year-old dining landmark with silver-trolley roasts and restored Edwardian interiors.

([Galerie Magazine](https://galeriemagazine.com/london-institution-simpsons-in-the-strand-returns-to-its-edwardian-glory/))

### **Bar Jamón** opens in San Francisco's Opera District

Two-time James Beard finalist **Ryan Pollnow** opens a Spanish tapas and gin bar one block from Davies Symphony Hall.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/bar-jamon-sf/))

### **Border Town** transitions from pop-up to permanent Austin shop

The breakfast taco stand, run by owners **Jesse ** and **Amanda Rodriguez**, settles into a brick-and-mortar location after operating as a roving vendor.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/09/dining/border-town-pop-up-breakfast-tacos.html))
