### Comey indictment may set a bad First Amendment precedent

Jacob Gaba argues the legal theory used against the former FBI director — even if successful here — opens the door to weaponized prosecutions over political speech regardless of which party holds power. The argument lands harder coming from the right because the structural concern (precedent doesn't care about the political identity of the next target) doesn't depend on agreeing with Comey. Worth tracking how this propagates across the legal-conservative commentariat: if it sticks, it narrows the political space for further indictments of similar shape. ([The Dispatch](https://thedispatch.com/article/comey-indictment-first-amendment-precedent/))

### Ukraine has been quietly clawing back ground

Morning Dispatch covers Ukrainian gains against Russia — incremental but consistent — alongside Trump's suspension of "Project Freedom" and a WHO advisory on a hantavirus outbreak. The Ukraine angle is under-covered in US media right now: the conflict has slipped below daily-news threshold here, but Ukrainian operations have meaningfully shifted in the last month, and the US policy posture (Project Freedom suspension) is moving in parallel. Two threads worth holding together. ([The Dispatch](https://thedispatch.com/newsletter/morning/ukraines-quiet-comeback-against-russia/))

### Why the unconverted MAGA base hasn't budged

Goldberg argues authenticity beats character or competence as the binding force inside Trump's coalition — written from inside the right with critical distance. The frame is useful regardless of where you sit politically: a thesis about what coalition-glue actually is in personality-driven political movements, and it travels to other contexts (sports loyalties, religious in-group dynamics, brand cults). Sharpest line: defections happen on character grounds, but defection-resistant loyalty is built on perceived authenticity, which has nothing to do with character. ([The Dispatch](https://thedispatch.com/article/trump-maga-loyalists-authenticity/))
