### **Oriana** opens in Nolita with wood-fire focus

**Oriana**, from **Andy Quinn** and **Cedric Nicaise** of **The Noortwyck**, debuts in Nolita centered on wood-fire cooking. The opening extends the team's track record following strong critical reception at their prior restaurant.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/05/oriana-nyc/))

### **LeoFigs** opens as winery-market-restaurant hybrid in Fishtown

**LeoFigs**, from spouses **Shannon Leocata Figueras** and **Justice Figueras**, combines natural wine production, retail market, and restaurant service in Philadelphia's Fishtown. The project represents a continued trend of vertically integrated food-wine operations in mid-Atlantic markets.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/05/leofigs-philly/))

### **Komal** review: **Fátima Júarez** centers Mexican antojitos at Mercado La Paloma

**Komal**, a molino and restaurant from chef **Fátima Júarez** inside **Mercado La Paloma** in Los Angeles, receives critical attention for its focused execution of Mexican antojitos. The review highlights the growing visibility of regional Mexican cooking in institutional food-hall formats.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/26/dining/restaurant-review-komal-los-angeles.html))

### **Bad Roman** expands to Beverly Hills, taking former **Palm Steakhouse** space

**Quality Branded**'s maximalist Italian concept **Bad Roman** opens in Beverly Hills at the iconic former **Palm Steakhouse** address, bringing **roni cups** and **pepperoncini martinis** to the West Coast. The expansion marks the restaurant group's continued national growth.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/bad-roman-beverly-hills/))

### **Gemma** opens on Waldorf Astoria Beverly Hills rooftop

**Gemma** launches as a Pan-Asian rooftop restaurant atop the **Waldorf Astoria Beverly Hills**, featuring **wagyu wontons** and a modern tiki bar with Hollywood Sign views. The opening adds to the concentrated luxury dining development along Wilshire corridor.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/gemma-waldorf-astoria-beverly-hills/))

### **La Pecora Bianca** expands to largest Broadway location

**La Pecora Bianca** opens its biggest outpost yet on Broadway in Manhattan, with handmade pastas, Neapolitan pizzas, and a draped ceiling installation. The expansion signals continued investor confidence in full-service Italian at scale in post-pandemic New York.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/la-pecora-bianca-nyc/))

### **Gusi** opens two-floor Eastern European destination in Greenwich Village

**Gusi**, from a husband-and-wife team, brings **borscht**, **puff pastry pierogi**, and **pine cone-infused vodkas** to a two-floor space in Greenwich Village. The opening adds to a small but notable resurgence of Eastern European programming in Manhattan below 14th Street.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/gusi-nyc/))

### Farm Bill's **'Save Our Bacon' Act** faces Senate hurdle

The **Save Our Bacon Act**, approved in the House farm bill, would preempt state authority to restrict meat sales based on animal welfare standards; **Senate Agriculture Committee Chair John Boozman** (R-Arkansas) has stated the language will not appear in the Senate draft expected early June. The provision represents a direct **National Pork Producers Council** priority conflicting with state-level regulations such as California's **Proposition 12**.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/05/28/the-farm-bill-is-no-place-for-big-porks-save-our-bacon-act/))
