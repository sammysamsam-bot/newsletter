### **Erewhon** launches $15,000-a-year Reserve VIP membership tier

The high-end grocer is testing a paid tier with dedicated shopping hours, priority delivery, and exclusive product access. Membership slots are capped and currently limited to Los Angeles.

([Eater](https://www.eater.com/groceries-retail/964871/erewhon-reserve-membership-perks))

### **Oriana** opens in Nolita with wood-fire focus

**Andy Quinn** and **Cedric Nicaise** of **The Noortwyck** debut **Oriana**, a Nolita restaurant centered on open-fire cooking. The opening follows their 2023 Times-reviewed debut.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/05/oriana-nyc/))

### **LeoFigs** opens as winery-market-restaurant hybrid in Philadelphia's Fishtown

**Shannon Leocata Figueras** and **Justice Figueras**'s **LeoFigs** combines natural wine production, retail market, and full-service dining under one roof in Fishtown.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/05/leofigs-philly/))

### **Bad Roman** takes over former Palm Steakhouse space in Beverly Hills

**Quality Branded**'s maximalist Italian concept **Bad Roman** opens at the iconic Beverly Hills address, replacing the longtime steakhouse with roni cups and pepperoncini martinis.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/bad-roman-beverly-hills/))

### **Gemma** opens on Waldorf Astoria Beverly Hills rooftop

Pan-Asian restaurant **Gemma** launches atop the Waldorf Astoria Beverly Hills with wagyu wontons, a modern tiki bar, and Hollywood Sign views.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/gemma-waldorf-astoria-beverly-hills/))

### **La Pecora Bianca** opens largest location on Broadway

The Italian restaurant group **La Pecora Bianca** debuts its biggest outpost yet on Broadway, with handmade pastas, Neapolitan pizzas, and a draped ceiling installation.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/la-pecora-bianca-nyc/))

### **Gusi** opens two-floor Eastern European restaurant in Greenwich Village

A husband-and-wife team bring **Gusi** to Greenwich Village with borscht, puff pastry pierogi, and pine cone-infused vodkas across two floors.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/gusi-nyc/))

### **Komal** reviewed: **Fátima Júarez** centers Mexican antojitos in Los Angeles

At **Komal**, inside Mercado La Paloma, **Fátima Júarez** runs a molino and restaurant focused on the rich tradition of Mexican antojitos. Pete Wells reviews.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/26/dining/restaurant-review-komal-los-angeles.html))

### **28 Nomad** opens with Hokkaido scallop Wellington from **Atomix** alumnus

New Manhattan restaurant **28 Nomad** features a Hokkaido scallop Wellington from a chef alum of **Atomix**; **The Silent Sea** also opens on City Island.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/26/dining/nyc-restaurant-news.html))

### House farm bill includes 'Save Our Bacon' Act overriding state animal welfare laws

The House-passed farm bill contains the **SOB Act**, which would preempt state authority to restrict meat sales based on animal welfare standards; Senate Agriculture Chair **John Boozman** says the provision won't appear in the Senate draft expected early June.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/05/28/the-farm-bill-is-no-place-for-big-porks-save-our-bacon-act/))
