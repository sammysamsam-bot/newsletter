### **Bad Roman** takes over former Palm Steakhouse space in Beverly Hills

**Bad Roman**, the maximalist Italian concept from **Quality Branded**, opens at the iconic former Palm address in Beverly Hills with pepperoncini martinis and roni cups. The expansion follows the brand's pattern of high-profile location takeovers.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/bad-roman-beverly-hills/))

### **Gemma** opens at Waldorf Astoria Beverly Hills rooftop

**Gemma**, a Pan-Asian rooftop restaurant, debuts at the Waldorf Astoria Beverly Hills with wagyu wontons, a modern tiki bar, and views of the Hollywood Sign. The opening marks another luxury hotel rooftop dining play in the competitive L.A. market.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/gemma-waldorf-astoria-beverly-hills/))

### **La Pecora Bianca** opens largest location on Broadway

**La Pecora Bianca**, the Italian restaurant group known for handmade pastas and Neapolitan pizzas, opened its biggest outpost yet on Broadway in Manhattan with a dramatic draped ceiling design. The expansion signals continued confidence in full-service Italian dining at scale.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/la-pecora-bianca-nyc/))

### **Gusi** brings Eastern European menu to Greenwich Village

**Gusi**, a two-floor Eastern European restaurant from a husband-and-wife team, opened in Greenwich Village serving borscht, puff pastry pierogi, and pine cone-infused vodkas. The opening adds to the neighborhood's diversifying dining landscape.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/gusi-nyc/))

### **Oriana** opens in Nolita with wood-fire focus

**Oriana**, from **The Noortwyck** team (**Andy Quinn**, **Cedric Nicaise**), opened in Nolita centering on wood-fire cooking. The restaurant arrives nearly three years after The Noortwyck's glowing New York Times review.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/05/oriana-nyc/))

### **LeoFigs** opens as winery-market-restaurant hybrid in Philadelphia's Fishtown

**LeoFigs**, combining a winery, market, and restaurant, opened in Philadelphia's Fishtown from **Shannon Leocata Figueras** and **Justice Figueras**. The multi-concept format reflects continued experimentation with hybrid food-retail models.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/05/leofigs-philly/))

### **The Silent Sea** opens on City Island; **28 Nomad** features Atomix alumnus

**The Silent Sea** brings more seafood to City Island in the Bronx, while **28 Nomad** in Manhattan features a **Atomix** alumnus serving Hokkaido scallop Wellington. The **Ludlow Room** also expanded seating for **Katz's Delicatessen**.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/26/dining/nyc-restaurant-news.html))

### **Komal** reviewed in Los Angeles

**Komal**, a molino and restaurant inside Mercado La Paloma from chef **Fátima Júarez**, received a New York Times review highlighting its focus on Mexican antojitos. The restaurant represents the deeper coverage of L.A.'s regional Mexican cooking.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/26/dining/restaurant-review-komal-los-angeles.html))

### Erewhon launches $15,000/year VIP membership tier

**Erewhon**, the Los Angeles luxury grocery chain, introduced **Erewhon Reserve**, a $15,000-per-year VIP membership with expanded perks. The program tests how far upscale grocery can push concierge-style subscription models.

([Eater](https://www.eater.com/groceries-retail/964871/erewhon-reserve-membership-perks))
