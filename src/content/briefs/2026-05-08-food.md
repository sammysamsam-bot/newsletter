### 2026 **James Beard Awards** finalists announced

The Beard Foundation released nominees across Outstanding Restaurant, Best New Restaurant, and regional Best Chef categories; winners will be revealed at a Chicago ceremony June 5. **Civil Eats** earned five media nominations, a record for the outlet.

([Eater](https://www.eater.com/dining-out/951811/2026-james-beard-awards-restaurant-chef-finalists) · [Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/05/06/civil-eats-nominated-for-a-record-five-james-beard-media-awards/))

### DOJ settles poultry price-fixing case with **Agri Stats**

The Department of Justice proposed a settlement with the agricultural data company, which was accused in a 2023 lawsuit of enabling poultry producers to coordinate price increases and supply decisions. The case marked rare federal antitrust enforcement against an information-sharing platform in meatpacking.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/05/08/doj-settles-poultry-price-fixing-case-with-agri-stats/))

### New USDA SNAP rule doubles required food varieties, critics skeptical

Retailers accepting SNAP benefits must now stock double the number of food varieties, but public health advocates say the measure won't meaningfully expand healthy options. The rule defines minimum varieties rather than nutritional quality.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/05/07/critics-say-new-snap-rule-will-do-little-to-increase-healthy-options/))

### **Oyatte** opens with single-farm sourcing model

**Oyatte**, a new restaurant from chef-owner **Henry Tan**, sources every ingredient from one Hudson Valley farm. The opening is part of a wave including **Gusi** (Slavic-Mediterranean) and rooftop bar **Bar Hugo**.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/05/dining/nyc-restaurant-news.html))

### **Delta Air Lines** ends snacks on short flights

The carrier will stop serving complimentary food and beverages on flights under 350 miles, while adding service to longer routes. The policy change affects most regional hops.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/05/travel/delta-airlines-flight-snack-policy-update.html))

### Supreme Court hears historic pesticide risk case

**Civil Eats** senior reporter **Lisa Held** covered oral arguments in a case challenging EPA pesticide approval processes, with protesters from the Make America Healthy Again movement demonstrating outside. The ruling could reshape how environmental risks are weighed against economic benefits in chemical regulation.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/05/06/inside-a-historic-supreme-court-case-on-pesticide-risks/))
