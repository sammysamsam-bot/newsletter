### Senate Democrats push to delay SNAP cost-shift mandate

Democrats on the Senate Agriculture Committee are fighting to strip a Republican-backed requirement that states pay a portion of **SNAP** benefits costs, included in the **One Big Beautiful Bill** (OBBB) now moving through Congress. The provision would end decades of full federal funding for the program.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/06/12/senate-democrats-continue-push-for-delay-on-snap-changes-for-states/))

### New World Screwworm outbreak spurs USDA pressure from Senate Democrats

Seven confirmed cases of **New World Screwworm** — a parasitic fly whose larvae consume living tissue — have prompted Senate Democrats to demand expanded **USDA** response and greater transparency on containment efforts. The outbreak threatens livestock and, in severe cases, human health.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/06/11/new-world-screwworm-outbreak-leads-senate-dems-to-push-for-usda-action/))

### **Noma** to reopen in Copenhagen with **René Redzepi** as creative director

The Copenhagen restaurant formerly named **World's Best Restaurant** will reopen after shuttering in 2024 amid reports of historical workplace abuse and a brief Los Angeles pop-up. **Redzepi** retains creative control but will not be in daily kitchen operations.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/10/dining/noma-reopening-copenhagen-rene-redzepi.html))

### **Bar Manje** brings Caribbean flavors to Manhattan's Upper West Side

The team behind longtime neighborhood diner **Good Enough to Eat** has converted the space into **Bar Manje**, a Caribbean-focused restaurant and bar near Central Park. The transition marks a full concept change for the decades-old address.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/bar-manje-nyc/))

### **Indienne** in Chicago pushes progressive Indian fine dining

Chef-owner **Sujan Sarkar** is expanding the scope of **Indienne**, his tasting-menu restaurant that applies modernist technique to Indian culinary traditions. The project is positioned among the most ambitious in Chicago's current dining landscape.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/indienne-chicago/))

### Little Tokyo's **Azay** continues under third-generation stewardship

**Philip Hirose**, 37, has taken over **Azay**, the Japanese restaurant his family has operated in Los Angeles's Little Tokyo for years. The handover follows family tragedy and tests whether the institution can maintain its place in a rapidly changing neighborhood.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/azay/))

### Bordeaux wine faces consumption crisis at home

Restaurants and wine bars in **Bordeaux** itself increasingly refuse to pour the region's namesake product, favoring other French and international wines. The trend points to deepening price and relevance challenges for the historic appellation.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/11/dining/drinks/bordeaux-wine.html))
