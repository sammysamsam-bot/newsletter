### **Noma** will reopen in Copenhagen with **René Redzepi** as creative director

The former "Best Restaurant in the World" returns following reports of past workplace abuse and a Los Angeles pop-up; **Redzepi** remains in a creative rather than day-to-day operational role.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/10/dining/noma-reopening-copenhagen-rene-redzepi.html))

### Senate Democrats push for delay on SNAP cost-sharing mandate

Democrats on the Agriculture Committee are seeking to strip the Republican **One Big Beautiful Bill** provision requiring states to pay a portion of **Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program** benefits; the change would shift billions in food assistance costs to state budgets.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/06/12/senate-democrats-continue-push-for-delay-on-snap-changes-for-states/))

### Senate Democrats press **USDA** on **New World Screwworm** outbreak response

Seven cases of the parasitic fly *(larvae feed on living tissue)* have been confirmed; lawmakers demand more aggressive federal action and transparency on containment efforts.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/06/11/new-world-screwworm-outbreak-leads-senate-dems-to-push-for-usda-action/))

### **Bar Manje** brings Caribbean flavors to Upper West Side

The new concept replaces neighborhood fixture **Good Enough to Eat** near Central Park, converting the longtime diner space into a Caribbean-focused restaurant.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/bar-manje-nyc/))

### **Indienne** and chef **Sujan Sarkar** mark Chicago expansion

The progressive Indian tasting menu restaurant, among the city's most ambitious, signals further growth from **Sarkar** as he builds on **Indienne's** critical reception.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/indienne-chicago/))

### **Azay** in Little Tokyo continues under new family stewardship

**Philip Hirose**, 37, has taken over the multigenerational restaurant following family tragedy, preserving the downtown Los Angeles Japanese dining destination.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/azay/))
