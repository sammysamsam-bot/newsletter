### China sanctions 10 US military-linked firms

China's Foreign Ministry announced counter-sanctions Monday against 10 American military-related companies, barring them from exports and investment ties. The move responds to US restrictions issued June 12 that blocked select Chinese tech firms from US defense contracts.

([Source](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/22/nx-s1-5866202/china-sanctions-restricting-exports))

### Ethiopian PM Abiy Ahmed's Prosperity Party wins parliamentary majority

Abiy Ahmed secured another term after his Prosperity Party won a landslide in parliamentary elections held June 21, according to provisional results. The victory gives the incumbent a fresh five-year mandate amid ongoing economic reform efforts and regional security challenges.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-06-22/ethiopian-pm-abiy-ahmed-s-party-wins-elections-video))

### Starmer expected to set resignation timetable

UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer could issue a statement ceding power as soon as Monday, according to allies cited by Bloomberg, though the timing remains uncertain. The departure would trigger a leadership contest likely to favor Andy Burnham and would mark Britain's seventh prime minister in a decade.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-06-22/starmer-could-set-out-resignation-timetable-imminently-video))
