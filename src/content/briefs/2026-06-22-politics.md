### China sanctions 10 U.S. military-linked companies

China's Ministry of Commerce announced retaliatory sanctions Monday on American defense contractors including Lockheed Martin Missiles and Fire Control and Raytheon Missiles and Defense, barring them from exports and investment in China. The move responds to U.S. restrictions barring certain Chinese tech firms from American defense contracts.

([Source](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/22/nx-s1-5866202/china-sanctions-restricting-exports))

### Ethiopia's Prosperity Party wins parliamentary elections

Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed secured a second term after his Prosperity Party won a landslide in parliamentary elections held June 21. The result gives Abiy a fresh five-year mandate; vote tallies and opposition seat counts were not immediately released by the National Electoral Board.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-06-22/ethiopian-pm-abiy-ahmed-s-party-wins-elections-video))

### U.S.-Iran technical talks continue in Switzerland

Mediators confirmed ongoing technical-level discussions toward a lasting peace deal, with President Trump simultaneously threatening strikes if Hezbollah maintains attacks on Israel. Israel's support is viewed by negotiators as crucial to advancing the interim agreement; no vote or signing date was announced.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-06-22/lebanon-remains-a-key-area-for-success-of-us-iran-talks-video))
