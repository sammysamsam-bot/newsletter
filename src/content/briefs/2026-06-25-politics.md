### Australian parliament passes housing tax overhaul

Australia's center-left government secured passage through parliament of legislation overhauling taxation of the property market. The changes are intended to reduce housing costs for younger Australians; the vote followed a period of declining property values that has wiped A$185 billion ($128 billion) off the nation's two largest housing markets this quarter.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-25/australian-government-secures-passage-of-housing-tax-overhaul))

### India's government set to approve Geely-backed manufacturing investment

India is preparing to approve a roughly $370 million investment from Horse Powertrain Ltd., a hybrid-engine venture backed by China's Zhejiang Geely Holding Group Co. The approval would mark one of the largest manufacturing investments from a Chinese-linked company in India in years, signaling a thaw in bilateral economic ties.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-25/india-set-to-approve-geely-backed-investment-as-china-ties-thaw))

### Japan's prime minister advances $2.3 trillion investment plan

Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi's government has put forward a $2.3 trillion investment plan, prompting warnings from bond strategists about renewed pressure on Japan's government bond market. The plan raises fresh concerns about fiscal sustainability and debt market stability.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-25/japan-s-2-3-trillion-investment-plan-raises-fresh-jgb-concerns))
