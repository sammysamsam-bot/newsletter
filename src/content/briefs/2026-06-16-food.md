### 2026 **James Beard Awards** winners announced

**Kalaya** *(Thai restaurant, Philadelphia)* took Outstanding Restaurant and **Michael Tusk** *(Quince, San Francisco)* won Outstanding Chef at the June 15 Chicago ceremony; immigration and resistance emerged as recurring themes in acceptance speeches.
