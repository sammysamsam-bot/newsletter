### 2026 **James Beard Awards** winners announced

**Kalaya** in Philadelphia won Outstanding Restaurant, and **Michael Tusk** *(Quince, San Francisco)* took Outstanding Chef at the Chicago ceremony on June 15. Immigration and resistance emerged as recurring themes in acceptance speeches. ([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/15/dining/2026-james-beard-restaurant-award-winners.html))

### **Sushi Nakazawa** debuts in Los Angeles

Chef **Daisuke Nakazawa** expands his omakase concept from New York's West Village to L.A., marking a long-anticipated West Coast arrival. Details on location and reservations remain limited. ([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/sushi-nakazawas-los-angeles/))

### **Zephyr Southern Brasserie** opens at Atlanta's Centennial Yards

A stylish French brasserie joins the still-developing 50-acre Centennial Yards district, part of the mixed-use site's gradual restaurant build-out. ([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/zephyr-atlanta/))

### **Gavroche** brings modern French fare to Chicago's Old Town

Restaurateur **Jason Chan** opens **Gavroche**, a French restaurant with playful sensibilities, in the Old Town neighborhood. ([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/gavroche-chicago/))
