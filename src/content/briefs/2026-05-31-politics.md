### DHS withdraws demand that green-card applicants leave US

The Department of Homeland Security reversed a policy announced May 23 that would have required most people seeking permanent legal residency to apply from outside the country. DHS stated Friday that "highly qualified and skilled" applicants will see "no noticeable impact," without defining the scope of the reversal or how many applicants are affected.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-30/dhs-backs-off-its-demand-for-green-card-applicants-to-leave-us))

### Japan's defense chief rejects China's "new militarism" accusation

Japanese Defense Minister Gen Nakatani dismissed China's characterization of Japan's military posture as "new militarism" during remarks Sunday at the Shangri-La Dialogue in Singapore, calling the label hypocritical.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-31/japan-rejects-china-s-new-militarism-label-as-hypocritical))

### Malaysia formally criticizes Norway for cancelled missile deal

Malaysia's government escalated its protest Saturday over Norway's termination of a weapons export agreement, with Defence Minister Mohamed Khaled Nordin stating the decision "erodes global trust" and demonstrates a "double standard" by stronger powers. Norway had cancelled the deal citing its interpretation of international humanitarian law obligations.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-31/malaysia-says-axed-noway-deal-hurts-trust-shows-double-standard))
