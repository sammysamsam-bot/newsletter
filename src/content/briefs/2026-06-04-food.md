### **Somssi**, from **Atoboy** and **Atomix** team, opens in Greenwich Village

**Somssi** *(pronounced somm-shi)* will bring Asian-informed bistro fare to the former Perry Street space in Greenwich Village, led by the **Junghyun Park**-backed group behind **Atomix** and **Atoboy**. The opening continues the group's expansion from Korean fine dining into broader formats.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/somssi-nyc/))

### **Alice & Monarch** opens in Cambridge with hidden dessert speakeasy

**Alice & Monarch**, a two-level Kendall Square concept from an **Eleven Madison Park**-trained chef, pairs a ground-floor restaurant with a basement dessert speakeasy. The dual-concept opening follows the speakeasy-dessert-bar trend already established in New York and Los Angeles.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/alice-monarch-cambridge/))

### USDA cuts dismantle Regional Food Business Centers

The Trump administration's USDA cuts have eliminated the **Regional Food Business Centers**, a network that supported local food economies through technical assistance and supply-chain coordination for small producers. Civil Eats reports the closures as part of a broader four-part series on local food system impacts.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/06/03/regional-food-business-centers-were-strengthening-local-food-economies-now-theyre-defunct/))

### **Lungi** brings southern Indian and Sri Lankan cooking to Upper East Side

**Lungi** opens as a New York destination for the cooking of southern India and Sri Lanka, joining a growing cluster of regional Indian restaurants that have moved beyond northern Indian canon in the city.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/02/dining/restaurant-review-lungi-nyc.html))
