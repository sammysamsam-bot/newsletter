### **Somssi**, from the team behind **Atoboy** and **Atomix**, opens in Greenwich Village

**Somssi** — a bistro concept with Asian sensibilities from the **Junghyun Park**-led team — is set to debut in Greenwich Village, combining classic bistro fare with the group's signature precision. The name is pronounced "somm-shi."

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/somssi-nyc/))

### USDA dismantles Regional Food Business Centers that supported local food economies

The Trump administration's cuts have defunded the **Regional Food Business Centers**, a USDA network that had been channeling resources to small farms and regional food hubs; Civil Eats reports the closures are already disrupting operations at affected farms. This is the third in a four-part series tracking the impact.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/06/03/regional-food-business-centers-were-strengthening-local-food-economies-now-theyre-defunct/))

### **Alice & Monarch** opens in Cambridge with dessert speakeasy below

An **Eleven Madison Park**-trained chef is behind **Alice & Monarch**, a two-level Kendall Square restaurant with a concealed dessert-focused speakeasy, **Monarch**, on its lower level.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/alice-monarch-cambridge/))

### **Lungi** brings southern Indian and Sri Lankan cooking to the Upper East Side

**Lungi** opens as a destination for the cooking of southern India and Sri Lanka, adding heat-focused regional specificity to a neighborhood not known for chile-driven dining.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/02/dining/restaurant-review-lungi-nyc.html))

### New York restaurant openings: luxury sushi, wood-fired grilling, and more
