### Film star Vijay forms government in India's Tamil Nadu state

South Indian actor C. Joseph Vijay's Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam party secured majority support from smaller parties to form government in Tamil Nadu, a key manufacturing hub for Apple iPhone and automobile production. The state election marks Vijay's transition from film to electoral politics.

([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-10/film-star-vijay-forms-new-government-in-india-s-key-iphone-hub))

### Far-right Australian party wins first lower house seat

A far-right party secured its first lower-house election victory in Australia, breaking through in a contest that highlighted declining support for the center-right coalition that has governed for much of the post-war period. Specific vote tallies were not immediately reported.

([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-10/far-right-australian-party-wins-seat-in-first-election-victory))

### Senate panel begins considering landmark digital asset bill as banks push late changes

Banking groups floated last-minute modifications to a compromise on stablecoin yield as a key Senate panel opened consideration of a landmark digital asset bill. The proposed changes come as the legislation, which would establish federal oversight of stablecoins, advances through committee.

([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-09/banks-and-crypto-backers-tussle-as-senators-eye-stablecoin-bill))
