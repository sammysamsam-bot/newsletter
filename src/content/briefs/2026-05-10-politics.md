### Tamil Nadu Swears In Film-Star-Led Government

C. Joseph Vijay's party secured majority support from smaller parties and formed the government in Tamil Nadu, India's industrialized southern state and a key hub for Apple iPhone and automobile production. The swearing-in follows state assembly elections.

([Bloomberg News](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-10/film-star-vijay-forms-new-government-in-india-s-key-iphone-hub))

### Far-Right Party Wins First Australian Lower-House Seat

A far-right party gained its first lower-house election victory in Australia, as vote counts from recent polls confirmed. The result comes amid declining support for the center-right coalition that has governed for much of the post-war period.

([Bloomberg News](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-10/far-right-australian-party-wins-seat-in-first-election-victory))

### Senate Panel Begins Considering Stablecoin Bill

The Senate Banking Committee started markup on a landmark digital-asset bill as banking groups floated last-minute changes to a compromise on stablecoin yield. The legislation would establish federal oversight of dollar-pegged cryptocurrencies.

([Bloomberg News](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-09/banks-and-crypto-backers-tussle-as-senators-eye-stablecoin-bill))
