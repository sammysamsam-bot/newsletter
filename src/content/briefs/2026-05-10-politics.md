### Brazil Supreme Court suspends law shortening Bolsonaro sentence

Justice Alexandre de Moraes suspended legislation that would reduce prison time for former President Jair Bolsonaro and others convicted of plotting a coup attempt. The ruling was released Saturday.

### Film star Vijay forms government in Tamil Nadu

C. Joseph Vijay's party secured support from smaller parties to reach majority and form the government in Tamil Nadu, one of India's most industrialized states and a key hub for automobile and iPhone production. The coalition was finalized following state elections.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-10/film-star-vijay-forms-new-government-in-india-s-key-iphone-hub))

### Malaysia studies action against Meta over fake royal accounts

Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil said the government is evaluating steps to take against Meta Platforms for failing to curb fake accounts involving Malaysia's Malay rulers, Bernama reported. No formal penalties have been announced.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-10/malaysia-mulls-steps-against-meta-on-fake-royal-accounts-report))
