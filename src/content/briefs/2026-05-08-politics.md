### Virginia Supreme Court rules against redistricting referendum

The Virginia Supreme Court threw out a Democrat-backed congressional map on Friday, blocking a referendum that would have asked voters to approve new district lines. The 4-3 ruling preserves Republican-favored boundaries for the 2026 midterms.

([Source](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/08/nx-s1-5816178/virginia-supreme-court-rules-against-voters-referendum-on-redistricting))

### New York Governor Hochul to participate in Trump's federal school choice program

Kathy Hochul announced Friday that New York State will opt into President Trump's $5 billion federal tax credit scholarship program, making New York the largest blue-state participant to date. The program offers tax credits to donors who fund private-school tuition.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-08/new-york-s-hochul-to-back-trump-s-tax-credit-for-school-choice))

### Trump shifts Iran strategy to prioritize reopening Strait of Hormuz

President Trump has resequenced his demands on Iran, pressing first for a ceasefire and safe passage of oil tankers through the Strait of Hormuz while deferring negotiations on Tehran's nuclear program and ballistic missile capabilities, administration officials said Friday.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-08/trump-punts-thorniest-iran-challenges-in-push-to-reopen-hormuz))
