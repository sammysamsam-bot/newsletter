### Pre-surgery immunotherapy keeps mismatch-repair-deficient colon cancer patients disease-free for 3 years

#### Trial outcomes
- Patients with mismatch-repair-deficient colorectal cancer received nine weeks of pembrolizumab before surgery in a UK-led trial.
- Nearly three years later, all patients remained cancer-free, with zero disease recurrences.
- The standard regimen—surgery followed by months of chemotherapy—was not required.

#### Practice implications
- A short preoperative immunotherapy course could replace surgery-first plus adjuvant chemo for this biomarker-defined subgroup.
- The finding challenges the dogma that surgery must come before systemic therapy, if confirmed in a randomized phase 3.

#### Context
The trial is small and single-arm; a randomized phase 3 is underway. But if results replicate, this shifts standard care for a sizable fraction of colon cancers. Watch for guideline updates and biomarker-testing expansion.
([ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/05/260505234618.htm))

### Ten-year trial: common knee meniscus surgery no better than placebo and may worsen arthritis

#### Findings
- A 10-year randomized trial compared partial meniscectomy to sham surgery for degenerative meniscus tears.
- No clinically meaningful difference in pain or function between the real-surgery and placebo groups ever emerged.
- Patients who had the real operation progressed to more severe symptoms, faster osteoarthritis, and more reoperations over a decade.

#### Clinical impact
- This definitive evidence contradicts a procedure performed hundreds of thousands of times annually, mostly without a placebo-controlled trial.
- Routine arthroscopic meniscectomy for non-traumatic tears should now be considered obsolete.

#### Context
Surgical culture and reimbursement incentives will slow adoption, but the data leave little room for debate. Expect professional societies to revise guidelines and health systems to restrict coverage soon.
([ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/05/260505234603.htm))

### New instruments finally reveal what triggers lightning, and storm clouds are stranger than we thought

#### New observational tools
- Radio-frequency interferometers and high-speed cameras capture lightning initiation in microsecond detail, identifying compact intracloud discharges as key precursors.
- Space-based gamma-ray detectors and ground arrays show that thunderstorms routinely emit terrestrial gamma-ray flashes, not just visible bolts.

#### Emerging mechanisms
- Cosmic-ray muons may provide the seed ionization that lets electric fields overcome air’s insulation, solving the long-standing “initiation problem.”
- Elves and sprites, brief optical emissions high above storms, have been imaged from the ISS, mapping electrical activity at the edge of space.

#### Context
For the first time, we’re seeing the full electromagnetic life of a thunderstorm, but a unified model linking these violent phenomena to a single lightning channel is still under construction.
([Quanta Magazine](https://www.quantamagazine.org/what-causes-lightning-the-answer-keeps-getting-more-interesting-20260506/))
