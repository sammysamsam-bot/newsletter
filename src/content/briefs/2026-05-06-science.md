### Pembrolizumab before surgery keeps colon cancer patients clear for nearly three years

A UK-led trial showed nine weeks of immunotherapy *before* surgery delivers sustained cancer-free outcomes for certain colorectal cancers — challenging the standard surgery-first sequencing. The result is part of a broader pattern: immunotherapy moving earlier in the treatment timeline, not later. If this generalizes, the rebuild of oncology surgical pathways is a multi-year project, and pharma incentives reshape accordingly. ([ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/05/260505234618.htm))

### A common knee surgery is ineffective and may worsen outcomes

A decade-long trial found meniscus trimming offers no benefit over placebo and correlates with faster osteoarthritis progression. The procedure is one of the most performed orthopedic surgeries globally — millions of cases a year — so a reversal at this evidence level reshapes a sizable chunk of musculoskeletal medicine. The interesting tension: surgical specialties have weak feedback loops compared to drug trials, and this is the kind of finding that shows what gets missed when the loop is loose. ([ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/05/260505234603.htm))
