### **Bertrand Grébaut**, influential French chef, dies at 44

**Bertrand Grébaut**, who opened **Septime** on Paris's Right Bank and helped steer French cooking toward vegetable-oriented cuisine, has died. The restaurant earned a Michelin star and shaped a generation of natural-wine-and-seasonal-vegetable dining.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/05/dining/bertrand-grebaut-dead.html))

### **All Well** opens in Chicago with "casual prix fixe" format

**All Well**, a new Chicago restaurant from the team behind **Daisies**, is testing a set-menu approach at lower price points — a bet that formal structure can coexist with relaxed dining. The opening is part of Eater's yearlong tracking of 2026's most anticipated restaurant launches.

([Eater](https://www.eater.com/pre-shift/972897/all-well-chicago-restaurant-pre-shift))

### **Oberon** opens at New York's expanded New Museum

The New Museum's **Oberon** pairs **OMA** architecture with **Julia Sherman**'s plant-focused cuisine and custom **Minjae Kim** furnishings, marking a rare museum restaurant launch with design and culinary co-signatures.

([Galerie Magazine](https://galeriemagazine.com/the-new-museum-debuts-a-cozy-design-forward-dining-destination/))

### **Brian Calvert** named editorial director at **Civil Eats**

**Brian Calvert**, former editor-in-chief of **High Country News**, takes over leadership of **Civil Eats**, the food-systems and agriculture-focused outlet. He oversaw growth and an Indigenous Affairs team at his previous post.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/07/06/longtime-environmental-journalist-brian-calvert-to-lead-civil-eats-as-editorial-director/))
