### **Bertrand Grébaut**, influential French chef, dies at 44

**Bertrand Grébaut**, who left a classical trajectory to open **Septime** on Paris's Right Bank and helped redefine French cooking through vegetable-forward, informally precise cuisine, has died. **Septime** became a global touchstone for a generation of chefs seeking to escape the stiffness of traditional haute cuisine.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/05/dining/bertrand-grebaut-dead.html))

### **All Well** opens in Chicago with "casual prix fixe" format

**All Well** is the city's buzziest new opening, built around a fixed-price menu served in a stripped-back setting — a bet that diners want the structure of tasting-menu dining without the formality. The launch is part of Eater's yearlong **Now Open** series tracking 2026's most notable restaurant debuts.

([Eater](https://www.eater.com/pre-shift/972897/all-well-chicago-restaurant-pre-shift))

### **Oberon** opens at the New Museum in New York

The expanded museum's full-service restaurant pairs **OMA** architecture with chef **Julia Sherman**'s plant-focused cooking and custom furnishings by **Minjae Kim**. It's a rare institutional dining debut with design-world stakes.

([Galerie Magazine](https://galeriemagazine.com/the-new-museum-debuts-a-cozy-design-forward-dining-destination/))

### **Brian Calvert** named editorial director of **Civil Eats**

Calvert, former editor-in-chief of **High Country News**, takes leadership of the food-systems publication after overseeing that newsroom's expansion and the launch of its Indigenous Affairs team. The move signals Civil Eats' continued pivot toward environmental and policy-facing coverage.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/07/06/longtime-environmental-journalist-brian-calvert-to-lead-civil-eats-as-editorial-director/))

### **Sunny's** in Miami draws Resy spotlight

**Sunny's**, already regarded as one of the city's most charmed dining rooms, is the subject of a Resy access feature — a signal of sustained heat for the spot.

### **1678 Omakase** opens affordable sushi counter in Atlanta
