### Trump nominates Todd Blanche as attorney general

President Trump announced at a White House dinner that he will formally nominate Todd Blanche, his personal criminal defense attorney and current deputy attorney general, to serve as attorney general. The announcement was first reported via video posted on social media by a White House aide.

([Source](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/04/nx-s1-5846307/trump-todd-blanche-ag))

### Israel and Lebanon reach ceasefire

Israel and Lebanon agreed to a ceasefire, brokered as the U.S. simultaneously seeks to revive diplomatic talks with Iran over its nuclear program. The agreement follows escalating cross-border hostilities tied to the broader regional conflict.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-06-04/israel-lebanon-reach-ceasefire-as-us-seeks-to-revive-iran-talks))

### Kim Jong Un inspects new nuclear facility, claims doubled output capacity

North Korean leader Kim Jong Un toured a newly unveiled nuclear materials production facility, with state media reporting the site doubles the country's capacity to produce weapons-grade material. The announcement coincides with reports that Chinese President Xi Jinping may travel to Pyongyang in coming weeks.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-04/kim-jong-un-tours-new-nuclear-site-says-output-capacity-doubled))

### European allies develop plan for Ukraine-Russia talks

Officials from Germany, France and the UK are working with Kyiv on a framework to engage Russia in negotiations to end the war in Ukraine, according to people familiar with the matter. The talks remain in preliminary stages with no fixed date.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-06-04/european-allies-sketch-plan-to-engage-putin-in-talks-video))

### Nigeria plans debt refinance amid elevated oil prices

Nigerian Finance Minister Taiwo Oyedele said the government will refinance high-cost debt and raise new funds to help plug its budget deficit, capitalizing on strong investor confidence driven by oil prices spiked by the U.S.-Iran conflict. Oyedele spoke to Bloomberg in London.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-06-04/nigeria-eyes-debt-refinance-amid-oil-spike-video))
