### Trump nominates Todd Blanche for attorney general

President Trump announced at a White House dinner that he will formally nominate Todd Blanche, who currently serves as deputy attorney general and was previously one of Trump's personal defense attorneys in the New York hush-money case.

### Israel and Lebanon reach ceasefire

Israel and Lebanon agreed to a ceasefire, with the U.S. simultaneously moving to revive direct talks with Iran over its nuclear program. The truce follows weeks of escalating cross-border strikes.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-06-04/israel-lebanon-reach-ceasefire-as-us-seeks-to-revive-iran-talks))

### Kim Jong Un unveils new nuclear facility, claims doubled output capacity

North Korean leader Kim Jong Un toured what state media described as a new nuclear materials production facility, asserting the country's weapons-grade material output capacity has doubled. The announcement coincides with satellite imagery showing construction at a Pyongyang site used for foreign leader visits, fueling speculation that Chinese President Xi Jinping may travel to North Korea for the first time since 2019.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-04/kim-jong-un-tours-new-nuclear-site-says-output-capacity-doubled))
