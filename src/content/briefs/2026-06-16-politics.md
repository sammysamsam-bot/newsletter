### Bank of Japan raises benchmark interest rate to 1%

Japan's central bank increased its benchmark rate to the highest level since 1995 and signaled further normalization ahead. The move continues the BOJ's gradual exit from decades of ultra-loose monetary policy.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-06-16/bank-of-japan-raises-benchmark-interest-rate-to-1-video))

### US, Europe disagree on Hormuz reopening timeline

President Trump stated the Strait of Hormuz will reopen by Friday; European allies at the G7 summit in Evian, France countered that oil and LNG flows cannot resume so quickly. The disagreement follows the US-Iran memorandum of understanding whose implementation details remain unresolved.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-06-16/us-europe-disagree-on-how-easy-it-is-to-reopen-hormuz))

### Greenland commander affirms existing base capacity sufficient

Denmark's top military commander in Greenland stated the territory has adequate bases and permanent troops to support NATO operations and deter Russian threats, even as Copenhagen's multi-billion-dollar Arctic defense expansion remains in early stages.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-16/greenland-has-enough-bases-to-defend-itself-commander-says))
