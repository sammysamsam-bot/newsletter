### Bank of Japan raises benchmark interest rate to 1%

Japan's central bank increased its policy rate to 1%, the highest level since 1995, and indicated further tightening ahead. Governor Kazuo Ueda announced the decision following the bank's two-day meeting in Tokyo.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-06-16/bank-of-japan-raises-benchmark-interest-rate-to-1-video))

### Trump says Strait of Hormuz will reopen by Friday; European allies disagree

At the G7 summit in Evian, France, President Donald Trump stated the Strait of Hormuz would resume oil and LNG flows by June 19. European officials present at the summit dispute that timeline, citing unresolved security and logistical conditions. Vice President JD Vance separately stated in a Fox News interview that Iran will "never get a dime of American taxpayer money" under the U.S.-Iran memorandum of understanding.

([Source 1](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-06-16/us-europe-disagree-on-how-easy-it-is-to-reopen-hormuz) · [Source 2](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-06-16/iran-won-t-get-a-dime-of-us-taxpayer-money-vance-says-video))

### Greenland's NATO commander says existing bases sufficient for Arctic defense

General Peter Boydsen, Denmark's top military commander in Greenland, stated that current bases and permanent troop levels on the island are adequate to support NATO operations and deter Russian threats. The assessment comes as Denmark pursues a multi-billion-dollar Arctic reinforcement program still in early stages.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-16/greenland-has-enough-bases-to-defend-itself-commander-says))
