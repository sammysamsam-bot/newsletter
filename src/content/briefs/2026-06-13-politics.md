### US military strikes kill Tren de Aragua leader in Venezuela

President Trump announced Friday that a U.S. strike killed Hector Rusthenford Guerrero Flores, identified as leader of the Venezuelan gang Tren de Aragua. The operation was carried out with Venezuelan government assistance, according to both U.S. and Bloomberg reports.

### US shoots down Iranian drones near Strait of Hormuz as interim deal talks continue

U.S. forces intercepted Iranian drones in the Strait of Hormuz on June 12 while negotiations for an interim agreement to reopen the waterway remained ongoing. The timeline for any finalized deal is unresolved.

([Bloomberg Politics](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-13/us-strikes-iranian-drones-while-talks-continue-for-interim-deal))

### ICC orders medical assessment of Rodrigo Duterte ahead of November trial

The International Criminal Court directed a new health evaluation of former Philippine President Rodrigo Duterte to determine fitness to stand trial for crimes against humanity charges linked to his anti-drug campaign. Trial proceedings are scheduled to begin in November 2026.

([Bloomberg Politics](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-13/icc-orders-another-health-check-for-ex-philippine-leader-duterte))

### Anthropic disables Mythos and Fable models following US foreign-access ban

Anthropic PBC suspended access to its Fable 5 and Mythos 5 AI models for all users, including domestic ones, after receiving a Trump administration directive barring foreign nationals from the systems. The company stated it is complying with the order.

([Bloomberg News](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-13/anthropic-says-us-limits-foreign-access-to-fable-5-mythos-5))

### Judge declines to block removal of Trump's name from Kennedy Center

A federal judge rejected requests to pause a court-ordered Friday deadline to strip President Trump's name from the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts. The center's appeal was also denied Friday evening; the institution must comply with the removal order.

([NPR National](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/12/g-s1-128017/trump-name-kennedy-center))
