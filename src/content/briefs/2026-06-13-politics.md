### Trump says U.S. strike killed leader of Tren de Aragua gang

President Trump announced Friday that a U.S. military strike killed Hector Rusthenford Guerrero Flores, identified as the leader of the Venezuelan Tren de Aragua gang. Trump said the strike was conducted with assistance from Venezuela.

([Source](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/12/nx-s1-5856611/u-s-military-kill-leader-tren-de-aragua))

### U.S. shot down Iranian drones near Strait of Hormuz

U.S. forces struck Iranian drones in the Strait of Hormuz on Friday as negotiations continued toward an interim deal to reopen the waterway. Timing of any agreement remains unresolved.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-13/us-strikes-iranian-drones-while-talks-continue-for-interim-deal))

### ICC orders medical assessment of former Philippine President Duterte

The International Criminal Court directed a new health evaluation of Rodrigo Duterte to determine fitness for trial; proceedings are scheduled for November.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-13/icc-orders-another-health-check-for-ex-philippine-leader-duterte))

### Federal judge declines to pause removal of Trump's name from Kennedy Center

A judge rejected a request to stop a court-ordered Friday deadline to remove Trump references from the Kennedy Center building; the institution's appeal was also denied Friday evening.

([Source](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/12/g-s1-128017/trump-name-kennedy-center))

### OpenAI under investigation by coalition of state attorneys general

A multi-state coalition of state attorneys general has requested information from OpenAI across a broad range of topics. The scope of the inquiry was not detailed in initial reports.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-13/openai-probed-by-coalition-of-state-attorneys-general))
