### Japan and Malaysia agree on deeper maritime defense cooperation

Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi and Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim signed agreements expanding defense and energy cooperation, citing shared concerns over China's maritime claims and economic disruptions from the Iran war. The meeting took place June 10 in Kuala Lumpur.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-10/japan-and-malaysia-agree-on-deeper-maritime-defense-cooperation))

### Young Koreans demand answers on ballot shortage as probe begins

South Korea's National Election Commission launched a probe after dozens of polling stations ran short of ballots during June 4 local elections, forcing some voters to leave and others to cast ballots after counting had begun. The shortage disproportionately affected young voters, who turned out in record numbers.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-10/young-koreans-demand-answers-on-ballot-shortage-as-probe-begins))

### Police, protesters clash in Kenya over US Ebola facility

Kenyan police fired teargas at demonstrators on June 10 protesting a planned quarantine facility for US servicemen and citizens exposed to Ebola, near Nairobi. The facility, reportedly intended for personnel evacuated from conflict zones, has drawn opposition from local residents and health workers.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-06-10/police-protesters-clash-in-kenya-over-us-ebola-facility-video))

### US launches strikes against Iran after helicopter shot down

President Donald Trump ordered retaliatory strikes against Iran after Tehran shot down a US military helicopter off the coast of Oman on June 9, according to a Bloomberg news brief. Oil prices rose following the exchange, which marks an escalation in the Gulf conflict.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-06-10/us-launches-strikes-against-iran-after-helicopter-shot-down))
