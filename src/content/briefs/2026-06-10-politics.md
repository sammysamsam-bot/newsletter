### US launches strikes against Iran after helicopter shot down

President Donald Trump ordered retaliatory strikes against Iran following Tehran's downing of an American military helicopter off the coast of Oman. The strikes mark an escalation in the ongoing conflict between the two countries.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-06-10/us-launches-strikes-against-iran-after-helicopter-shot-down))

### Japan and Malaysia agree on deeper maritime defense cooperation

Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi and Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim signed agreements to expand defense and energy cooperation, with both leaders citing shared concerns over China's maritime claims and economic disruption from the Iran war. The deal was finalized June 10 in Kuala Lumpur.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-10/japan-and-malaysia-agree-on-deeper-maritime-defense-cooperation))

### Afghanistan reports 13 killed in new Pakistan cross-border strikes

Afghanistan's government stated that Pakistani military strikes overnight in eastern provinces killed 13 people, including children. The incident renews months of cross-border clashes that have left hundreds dead on both sides.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-10/afghanistan-says-13-killed-in-new-pakistan-cross-border-strikes))
