### Huberman + Dr. Paul Conti — strengths-over-deficits framing for mental health

Conti's approach inverts the standard clinical model: lead with existing strengths and self-reflection questions, treat consistent action as more durable than introspection. The practical takeaway from the episode is a small set of weekly self-check questions ("when did I act with integrity," "where did I avoid something I should have done") used as a behavioral feedback loop rather than as therapeutic mining of past wounds. Compatible with both clinical depth and lighter self-management — useful framing whether you have a therapist or not.
