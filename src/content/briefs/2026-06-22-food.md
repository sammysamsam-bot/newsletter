### A Juneteenth festival honors **Laura "Dolly" Johnson**, 19th-century White House chef

**Laura "Dolly" Johnson**, an African American chef who served four presidents starting with Benjamin Harrison in 1889, is the subject of a Juneteenth festival recognizing her two-administration tenure. The event spotlights a figure historians are still working to fully document from a single surviving circa-1890 photograph.
