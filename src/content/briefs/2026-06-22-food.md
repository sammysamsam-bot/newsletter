### A Juneteenth festival honors 19th-century White House chef **Laura "Dolly" Johnson**

The celebration spotlights Johnson, an African American chef who served four presidents across two administrations starting with Benjamin Harrison in 1889. Historians continue to debate the attribution of a circa-1890 photograph believed to depict her.
