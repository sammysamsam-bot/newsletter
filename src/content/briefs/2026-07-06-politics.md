### Platner says he will "reflect on the best path forward" after new assault allegation

Graham Platner, Democratic nominee for Maine's U.S. Senate seat against incumbent Susan Collins, posted a social media video stating he would consider whether to remain in the race following a new sexual assault allegation published by Politico, which he denied.

### Trump travels to NATO summit in Turkey

President Donald Trump is en route to the NATO summit meeting in Turkey. The trip follows his ringing the opening bell for the NYSE and Nasdaq from the Oval Office earlier today to mark the launch of Trump Accounts, a new investment vehicle for children.
