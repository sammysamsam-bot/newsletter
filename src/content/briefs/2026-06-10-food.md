### **Wokuni** opens Midtown outpost with Nagasaki tuna farm

**Wokuni**, a Japanese seafood restaurant, opened its second New York location in Midtown West with live tuna carving, whole-fish auctions, and an attached fish-market retail counter. The group owns and operates its own tuna farm in Nagasaki.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/wokuni-nyc/))

### **Eddie V's** expands to Naples

Darden-owned seafood chain **Eddie V's** opened in Naples, Florida, with nightly live music and daily flown-in seafood.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/eddie-vs-naples/))

### **The Capital Grille** arrives in suburban Detroit

**The Capital Grille** opened in Livonia, Michigan, with a Detroit-area native running the dining room and a 3,500-bottle wine cellar.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/the-capital-grille-livonia/))

### **Rye Bunny** replaces Tail Up Goat in D.C.

**Rye Bunny**, from **Tail Up Goat** co-owners **Jon Sybert** and **Jill Tyler**, took over the acclaimed D.C. space with a counter-service-hybrid model focused on casual comfort dishes.

([Eater](https://www.eater.com/dining-out/967886/rye-bunny-takes-over-the-tail-up-goat-space-with-casual-comfort-dishes))

### **Post Modern Times** in Minneapolis stops charging for food

**Post Modern Times** cafe in Minneapolis eliminated menu prices as a tax protest turned social experiment; the operation reports profits are up under the voluntary-pay model.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/08/dining/post-modern-times-minneapolis-free-food.html))

### **Sqirl** launches dinner service in Los Angeles

**Sqirl**, the Los Angeles restaurant that survived 2023's "JamGate" preservation scandal, is now open for dinner service years after building its reputation as a daytime brunch destination.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/09/dining/restaurant-review-sqirl-los-angeles.html))

### **Fireboat** joins NYC's floating bar fleet

**Fireboat** is among new floating bars opening on New York waterways this season, alongside **Malvan** (Goan cuisine) and **Forno d'Oro** (Naples-trained pizza).

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/09/dining/nyc-restaurant-news.html))
