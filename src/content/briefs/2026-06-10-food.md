### **Fireboat** joins NYC's floating bar fleet, plus **Malvan** and **Forno d'Oro** open

**Fireboat**, a bar on a former FDNY vessel, docks at Pier 25 in Tribeca; also opening: **Malvan** (Goan cuisine, East Village) and **Forno d'Oro** (pizza with pedigree, West Village). ([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/09/dining/nyc-restaurant-news.html))

### **Post Modern Times** in Minneapolis drops menu prices to zero — profits rise

The cafe stopped charging for food as a tax protest and hospitality-industry critique; revenue from drinks and voluntary contributions now exceeds prior sales. ([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/08/dining/post-modern-times-minneapolis-free-food.html))

### **Rye Bunny** replaces **Tail Up Goat** in D.C. with counter-service comfort food

Co-owners **Jon Sybert** and **Jill Tyler** converted the acclaimed fine-dining space into a hybrid counter-service model. ([Eater](https://www.eater.com/dining-out/967886/rye-bunny-takes-over-the-tail-up-goat-space-with-casual-comfort-dishes))

### **Sqirl** expands to dinner service in Los Angeles

**Jessica Koslow**'s Silver Lake restaurant, previously brunch-only and years past its "JamGate" controversy, now operates evenings. ([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/09/dining/restaurant-review-sqirl-los-angeles.html))

### **Wokuni** opens second NYC location in Midtown with tuna farm supply chain

The Japanese seafood spot, which owns its tuna farm in Nagasaki, features live carving, whole-fish auctions, and retail fish market. ([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/wokuni-nyc/))

### **The Capital Grille** opens in Livonia, Michigan

Darden-owned steakhouse arrives in Detroit suburbs with a 3,500-bottle wine cellar and a Detroit-area native running the dining room. ([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/the-capital-grille-livonia/))

### **Eddie V's** expands to Naples, Florida

Darden's upscale seafood chain opens its latest location with daily flown-in seafood and nightly live music. ([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/eddie-vs-naples/))
