### Trump announces 5,000 additional US troops to Poland

President Donald Trump announced the deployment of 5,000 additional American troops to Poland during a NATO summit appearance. NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte welcomed the pledge, which reverses prior signals of US drawdowns in Europe.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-22/nato-welcomes-new-trump-troop-pledge-as-allies-caught-off-guard))

### US sanctions Tanzanian police chief over alleged torture of activists

The Treasury Department imposed sanctions on Camillus Wambura, Tanzania's Inspector General of Police, for his alleged role in the torture of detained human-rights activists ahead of the country's disputed 2025 presidential elections.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-22/us-sanctions-tanzanian-police-chief-over-alleged-rights-abuses))

### UK posts highest April budget deficit since pandemic

UK government borrowing reached £25.5 billion in April, the highest for that month since 2021, driven by energy-cost pressures from the Iran war and elevated debt-servicing costs.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-22/uk-posts-highest-april-budget-deficit-since-the-pandemic))
