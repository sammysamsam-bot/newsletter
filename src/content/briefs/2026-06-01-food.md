### **28 Nomad** opens in NYC with Hokkaido scallop Wellington from Atomix alum

**28 Nomad**, from chef **Jae Lee** *(formerly of **Atomix**)*, debuted with a Hokkaido scallop Wellington as its signature dish. Also opening: **The Silent Sea** on City Island for seafood, and **The Ludlow Room**, an expanded seating annex for **Katz's Delicatessen**.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/26/dining/nyc-restaurant-news.html))

### Trump stock purchases include **John Deere** and major food companies

A Q1 2026 financial disclosure filed with the Office of Government Ethics shows President Trump's portfolio managers bought stock in **John Deere**, **Cargill**, **Tyson Foods**, and **PepsiCo** — companies that have had direct dealings with his administration. The purchases raise standard conflict-of-interest questions given pending USDA and FTC matters affecting those firms.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/05/29/trump-stock-purchases-include-john-deere-and-major-food-companies/))

### Dates become snack-aisle star as fiber-focused brands multiply

**Joolies**, **Date Better**, and **Bard Valley Natural Delights** are among the brands rolling out stuffed, coated, and blended date products; the category surge follows similar trajectory of earlier "it" snacks like seaweed and chickpea puffs, with actual nutritional density as the selling point this time.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/29/dining/fiber-rich-dates-snacks.html))
