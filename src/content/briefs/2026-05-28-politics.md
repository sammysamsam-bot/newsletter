### Philippines anti-graft body charges Senator Jinggoy Estrada with plunder

The Philippines' Ombudsman filed plunder and graft charges against Senator Jinggoy Estrada for alleged involvement in a multibillion-peso flood infrastructure corruption scheme. The charges carry potential penalties of life imprisonment for plunder.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-28/philippines-charges-senator-over-flood-infrastructure-scandal))

### Trump files new $10 billion libel suit against Wall Street Journal

President Donald Trump submitted a revised complaint in his $10 billion lawsuit against the Wall Street Journal and News Corp. over an article on his alleged ties to Jeffrey Epstein, after a judge dismissed an earlier version. The filing was made in federal court on May 27.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-28/trump-files-new-10-billion-lawsuit-against-wall-street-journal))

### Vietnam and Thailand agree to deepen security and technology ties

Vietnamese leader To Lam and Thai officials signed cooperation agreements on security, defense, technology sharing, and green energy during Lam's first visit to Thailand as Vietnam's top leader on May 27–28. The agreements follow a period of warming ties between the two ASEAN members.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-28/vietnam-thailand-agree-to-deepen-ties-in-to-lam-s-first-visit))

### US imposes fresh sanctions on Iran as Gulf attacks resume

The Treasury Department announced new sanctions targeting Iran as part of a renewed pressure campaign, coinciding with resumed attacks in the Persian Gulf that have disrupted regional oil flows. Millions of barrels of daily supply remain shut-in across the region.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-05-28/oil-jumps-as-gulf-attacks-threaten-ceasefire-video))
