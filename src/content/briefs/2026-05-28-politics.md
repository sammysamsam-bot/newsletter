### Sweden to sell, donate 36 Saab Gripen jets to Ukraine

Sweden agreed to sell 20 of the latest-model Saab Gripen fighter jets to Ukraine and donate 16 older variants, a major defense transfer as Kyiv continues operations against Russian forces. The deal was announced May 28.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-28/sweden-to-sell-donate-saab-jets-to-ukraine-to-aid-defense))

### Russia and Kazakhstan sign energy, currency-swap deals during Putin visit

Russian President Vladimir Putin visited Kazakhstan on May 28, where the two countries signed agreements including a currency-swap arrangement and Russian financing for Kazakhstan's first nuclear power plant.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-28/russia-and-kazakhstan-sign-energy-fx-swap-deals-as-putin-visits))

### Hungary's Magyar says deal 'very close' to unlock suspended EU funds

Hungarian Prime Minister Peter Magyar said May 28 his government is near an agreement with the European Union that would release billions of euros in suspended aid, easing pressure on Hungary's budget.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-28/hungary-is-very-close-to-eu-funding-deal-premier-magyar-says))
