### Russia bans jet fuel exports through November

Russia prohibited exports of jet fuel effective immediately, extending through end-November, after Ukrainian attacks on domestic refineries intensified. The ban follows sustained strikes on energy infrastructure that Moscow says threaten supply security.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-01/russia-bans-jet-fuel-exports-as-attacks-on-refineries-intensify))

### US strikes Iran drone sites as negotiators trade draft agreements

US forces hit Iranian drone facilities over the weekend, President Donald Trump confirmed, even as the two countries exchanged draft language for an interim deal to extend ceasefire terms and reopen the Strait of Hormuz. The strikes and talks proceeded concurrently near the Gulf waterway.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-01/us-hits-iran-drone-sites-over-weekend-as-two-sides-trade-drafts-on-deals))

### French navy boards second shadow-fleet tanker in Russian oil crackdown

France's navy intercepted another oil tanker on the high seas after it departed from Russia, continuing enforcement against vessels used to circumvent sanctions on Russian crude exports. The boarding follows a pattern of increased maritime interdiction in the shadow-fleet trade.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-01/france-boards-another-tanker-tied-to-russian-oil-trade))

### Philippine court orders second arrest of Senator Estrada in graft scandal

A Philippine court issued an arrest warrant for Senator Jinggoy Estrada on plunder and graft charges, days after he posted bail in a separate case tied to the same flood-infrastructure corruption scandal. The new order compounds legal pressure on Estrada, who faces multiple counts in the nationwide probe.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-01/philippine-senator-faces-second-arrest-order-over-graft-scandal))

### Israel expands Lebanon ground assault to broadest incursion in 25 years

Israeli Defense Minister Israel Katz announced IDF forces planted a flag at the historic Beaufort castle near Nabatieh, declaring the expanded operation a "permanent presence" in southern Lebanon. The ground assault represents Israel's widest incursion into Lebanon since 2000, conducted as Hezbollah intensified attacks on Israel's northern border.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-06-01/israel-expands-operation-in-lebanon-video))
