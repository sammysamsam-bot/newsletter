### Russia bans jet fuel exports through end-November

Russia prohibited jet fuel exports through November 30 after Ukrainian strikes damaged domestic refineries and created shortage risk at home. The ban follows repeated drone attacks on Russian energy infrastructure in recent weeks.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-01/russia-bans-jet-fuel-exports-as-attacks-on-refineries-intensify))

### US strikes Iran drone sites as negotiators exchange draft deal terms

US forces hit Iranian drone facilities over the weekend while both sides continued exchanging draft language for an interim agreement, President Donald Trump said. The strikes and talks proceeded concurrently near the Strait of Hormuz, where shipping remains disrupted.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-01/us-hits-iran-drone-sites-over-weekend-as-two-sides-trade-drafts-on-deals))

### Philippine court orders Senator Jinggoy Estrada arrested on plunder and graft charges

A Philippine court issued a second arrest order for Senator Jinggoy Estrada on plunder and graft charges tied to a flood infrastructure corruption scandal. The order came days after Estrada posted bail in a separate related case.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-01/philippine-senator-faces-second-arrest-order-over-graft-scandal))

### France boards another shadow-fleet tanker in Russian oil crackdown

The French navy boarded an oil tanker on the high seas after it departed Russia, continuing enforcement against shadow-fleet vessels used to circumvent sanctions on Russian crude exports.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-01/france-boards-another-tanker-tied-to-russian-oil-trade))
