### CBO estimates Trump's 'Golden Dome' at $1.2 trillion

The Congressional Budget Office released an analysis estimating the missile defense program would cost $1.2 trillion over 20 years, far above the initial $175 billion price tag. The report was published Tuesday.

([Source](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/13/g-s1-121780/trumps-golden-dome-cost))

### UK Labour PM Starmer retains leadership after 90 MPs call for resignation

Keir Starmer told his Cabinet at a Downing Street meeting Tuesday that he would not discuss his future there and dared opponents to challenge him individually. Some 90 Labour MPs had publicly called for him to step down; no ministerial resignations have forced his departure.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-05-13/starmer-survives-as-potential-successors-bide-their-time-video))

### Philippines House impeaches Vice President Sara Duterte

The House of Representatives voted to impeach Vice President Sara Duterte, the second impeachment proceeding against her. The move escalates the break between the Duterte and Marcos political dynasties that had allied in the 2022 election.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-13/why-was-philippine-vp-sara-duterte-impeached-again-what-happens-next))
