### Congressional Budget Office estimates 'Golden Dome' at $1.2 trillion

The CBO released analysis Wednesday projecting President Trump's proposed missile defense shield would cost $1.2 trillion over 20 years, far above the administration's initial $175 billion estimate. No vote or markup has been scheduled.

([Source](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/13/g-s1-121780/trumps-golden-dome-cost))

### Trump to meet Xi at summit

The White House confirmed Wednesday that President Trump will hold a summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping, with timing and location undisclosed.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-05-13/trump-to-meet-xi-for-high-stakes-us-china-summit-video))

### UK Prime Minister Starmer retains leadership amid Labour revolt

Approximately 90 Labour MPs have called for Keir Starmer to step down following Tuesday's ministerial resignations. Starmer told Cabinet members at Downing Street Tuesday that leadership discussions would happen individually, not in meeting; no formal challenge has materialized.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-05-13/starmer-survives-as-potential-successors-bide-their-time-video))
