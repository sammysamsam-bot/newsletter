### US and Iran postpone technical peace talks in Switzerland

US Vice President JD Vance delayed his planned Friday travel to Switzerland after the US and Iran postponed the start of negotiations over a permanent peace deal and nuclear restrictions. Vance told reporters Thursday he expected talks to begin sometime during the weekend.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-06-19/vance-delays-swiss-trip-for-iran-talks-video))

### UK government borrowing hits highest May level since pandemic

Debt-interest costs pushed UK government borrowing to its highest level for any May in six years, according to figures released Thursday. The fiscal data lands as Labour Party figures including Greater Manchester Mayor Andy Burnham position themselves as potential successors to Prime Minister Keir Starmer.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-19/uk-posts-highest-budget-deficit-for-any-may-since-the-pandemic))

### US imposes visa restrictions on Tigray leaders in Ethiopia

The State Department announced visa restrictions Thursday on several members of the Tigray People's Liberation Front, citing actions threatening peace and stability in Ethiopia and the broader Horn of Africa.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-19/us-issues-visa-restrictions-for-some-tigray-leaders-in-ethiopia))

### Burnham wins Labour hold in Makerfield by-election

Greater Manchester Mayor Andy Burnham won the Makerfield parliamentary by-election for Labour with 54.8% of the vote, defeating Reform UK's Robert Kenyon on 34.5%. The result marks Burnham's return to Parliament and positions him to challenge for Labour leadership.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-06-19/burnham-s-by-election-win-paves-way-to-oust-starmer-video))

### Malaysia overhauls civil servant asset rules

Malaysia's government reworked rules governing civil servants' shareholdings and asset declarations, months after public disclosure of former anti-graft chief Azam Baki's stock holdings triggered national debate.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-19/malaysia-reworks-asset-rules-for-officials-after-azam-scandal))
