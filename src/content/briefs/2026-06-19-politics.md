### US issues visa restrictions on Tigray leaders

The State Department imposed visa restrictions on several members of Ethiopia's Tigray People's Liberation Front, citing threats to peace and stability in the Horn of Africa. The specific individuals were not named.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-19/us-issues-visa-restrictions-for-some-tigray-leaders-in-ethiopia))

### Zimbabwe parliament backs constitutional amendment to extend presidential terms

Zimbabwe's parliament approved a constitutional amendment that would lengthen presidential terms and allow 83-year-old President Emmerson Mnangagwa to remain in office beyond 2028.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-06-19/zimbabwe-backs-plan-to-extend-mnangagwa-rul-video))

### Malaysia overhauls civil servant asset rules after anti-graft chief scandal

Malaysia revised rules governing civil servants' shareholdings and asset declarations, months after a report on former anti-graft chief Azam Baki's stock holdings triggered public debate.

([Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-19/malaysia-reworks-asset-rules-for-officials-after-azam-scandal))
