### **Marty Makary** out as FDA Commissioner; **Kyle Diamantas** steps up as acting

**Marty Makary** is resigning as FDA Commissioner; **Kyle Diamantas**, the agency's top food official, becomes acting commissioner. The move leaves both FDA and CDC without Senate-confirmed leadership.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/05/12/marty-makary-out-as-fda-commissioner/))

### **Cafe Bar J.F.** opens in North Brooklyn as South American tavern

**Juan Correa** and **Erik Ramirez** of **Llama Inn** open **Cafe Bar J.F.** in Williamsburg, modeled on the informal *fondas* and *cantinas* of Peru, Colombia, and Argentina. The corner space functions as a third-space tavern with all-day hours.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/05/cafe-bar-jf-nyc/))

### **Sanwits** opens in East Village with Filipino sandwiches

**Anton Dayrit** and **Unapologetic Foods** launch **Sanwits**, a fast-casual Filipino sandwich shop at 32 Avenue A with seven styles including *tortang talong* and Spam variants.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/12/dining/nyc-restaurant-news.html))

### **Dean's Pub** becomes New York's buzziest new opening

**Dean's Pub** in the West Village, from the team behind **Hart's** and **Cervo's**, has emerged as the city's most in-demand new restaurant despite — or because of — its pub format. The kitchen is led by **Nick Perkins** and **Maya Wright**.

([Eater](https://www.eater.com/pre-shift/961028/deans-pub-new-york-pre-shift))

### **Simon Kim** triple-concept takeover at 550 Madison

**Simon Kim** of **Cote** opens three concepts inside Philip Johnson's landmark tower: **Bar Chimera** (cocktail bar), **Cote 550** (Korean steakhouse), and **Sushi Yoshitake** (Tokyo import, third location outside Japan). A significant Midtown dining consolidation.

([Galerie Magazine](https://galeriemagazine.com/simon-kim-stages-a-triple-concept-takeover-of-550-madison/))

### **José Andrés**'s **Zaytinya** opens first Silicon Valley outpost in Palo Alto

James Beard Award winner **José Andrés** expands his **ThinkFoodGroup** with **Zaytinya** in Palo Alto, the Mediterranean mezze concept's debut in the Bay Area.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/zaytinya-palo-alto/))

### New York state legislation would ban **bromated flour**, threatening bagel and pizza economics

A pending ban on potassium bromate, a carcinogenic dough conditioner used in commercial baking, would force reformulation of New York's characteristic elastic, low-cost bagel and pizza crusts. The additive is already banned in Europe, California, and Canada.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/11/dining/bromated-flour-ban-new-york-pizza-bagels.html))
