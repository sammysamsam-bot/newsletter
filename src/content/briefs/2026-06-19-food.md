### Here Are the 2026 **James Beard Restaurant Award** Winners

**Kalaya** in Philadelphia won Outstanding Restaurant and **Michael Tusk** of San Francisco's Quince took Outstanding Chef at a Chicago ceremony where immigration and resistance surfaced as recurring themes.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/15/dining/2026-james-beard-restaurant-award-winners.html))

---

### **This Red Rooster** opens as living tribute to Miami's Black History

**Red Rooster** debuts in Miami's Overtown neighborhood as a restaurant, music venue, and cultural archive honoring the area's historic Black entertainment district; the opening is among a wave of new Miami spots surfacing for World Cup season.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/red-rooster-miami/))

---

### **Albi** earns four stars in Washington, D.C.

The **Michael Rafidi**-led Palestinian restaurant **Albi** received a four-star NYT review for its bread-and-fire cooking in the nation's capital, a rare top-tier rating that marks Rafidi as a defining voice in regional Middle Eastern cuisine in the U.S.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/16/dining/restaurant-review-albi-washington-dc.html))

---

### **Trudie's Tavern** opens in Carroll Gardens

The **Gertie** team, led by restaurateur **Nate Adler**, expands to Brooklyn with **Trudie's Tavern**, a neighborhood bar and restaurant opening this week in the former Ferry's Landing space on Henry Street.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/trudies-tavern-nyc/))

---

### **Titán** brings Northern Mexican cantina cooking to New York

**Titán** opens in Manhattan with a menu focused on the grilled meats and flour tortillas of northern Mexico, a regional style still underrepresented in the city's increasingly deep Mexican restaurant scene.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/titan-nyc/))
