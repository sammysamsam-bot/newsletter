### Here Are the 2026 James Beard Restaurant Award Winners

**Kalaya** in Philadelphia won Outstanding Restaurant and **Michael Tusk** *(Quince, San Francisco)* took Outstanding Chef at the June 13 Chicago ceremony. Immigration and resistance surfaced as recurring themes in acceptance speeches.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/15/dining/2026-james-beard-restaurant-award-winners.html))

### **Titán** opens as Northern Mexican cantina in New York

**Titán**, a Northern Mexican-focused cantina, is now open in New York with a regional cooking emphasis distinct from typical city Mexican offerings. The opening adds to a recent wave of depth-first regional Mexican restaurants in the market.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/titan-nyc/))

---

### **Red Rooster** opens in Miami as living tribute to Black history

**Red Rooster**, Marcus Samuelsson's Harlem-born concept, debuts in Miami's Overtown neighborhood with design and programming honoring the area's Black cultural legacy — the historic corridor once hosted Muhammad Ali, Cab Calloway, and Aretha Franklin. The opening marks Samuelsson's return to the brand after its original Harlem location closed in 2024.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/red-rooster-miami/))

### **Albi** earns four stars from NYT in Washington, D.C.

**Michael Rafidi's** Palestinian restaurant **Albi** in Washington, D.C. received a four-star review for its bread-and-fire-driven menu told through a Palestinian narrative. Rafidi, also behind **Yellow** nearby, centers Palestinian ingredients and techniques at the Georgetown location.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/16/dining/restaurant-review-albi-washington-dc.html))

---

### **The Gertie Team** expands to Carroll Gardens with **Trudie's Tavern**

Restaurateur **Nate Adler** *(Gertie, Brooklyn)* opens **Trudie's Tavern** in Carroll Gardens, a neighborhood tavern follow-up to his all-day café **Gertie**. The project was conceived during a 2025 Los Angeles trip, per Adler.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/trudies-tavern-nyc/))
