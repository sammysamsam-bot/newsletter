### **Capitaine** opens in West Village with tavern-style seafood

**Capitaine** joins a busy week of tavern openings in Manhattan alongside **Grimm Tavern** from the beer brand and **Trudie's Tavern** from the Gertrude's owners. The new restaurant focuses on seafood interpretations in the West Village.

([NYT Dining](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/23/dining/nyc-restaurant-news.html))

### **Asia Live** opens as immersive Asian food emporium in Silicon Valley

**Asia Live** debuts in Santa Clara with regional flavors, distinct bars, and open kitchen theater, marking a notable new entry in the Bay Area dining scene.

([OpenTable Blog](https://www.opentable.com/blog/asia-live-santa-clara/))

### **Longman & Eagle** names new head chef, Logan Square tavern remains unchanged

**Longman & Eagle**, the Chicago gastropub that defined the 2010s rustic-tavern wave, has installed a new head chef while keeping its moody, time-worn atmosphere intact.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/longman-eagle/))

### **Mister Jiu's** marks 10 years in San Francisco's Chinatown

**Brandon Jew's** **Mister Jiu's** celebrates its decade anniversary, having opened in 2015 in the country's oldest Chinatown and becoming a defining modern Chinese-American restaurant.

([Resy Blog](https://blog.resy.com/2026/06/mister-jius-10-year-anniversary/))

### **USDA** moves to hire back farm office staff after year of cuts

After more than a year of workforce reductions, the **U.S. Department of Agriculture** says it is hiring to fill vacancies in county offices that directly serve farmers. The reversal acknowledges staffing gaps that had left field offices under-resourced.

([Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/06/24/usda-says-it-is-working-to-address-farm-office-staff-shortages/))
